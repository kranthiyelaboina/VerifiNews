How It Works

Our verification pipeline processes your input through multiple stages to determine authenticity

01

Input Statement

Enter any news headline, claim, or upload a newspaper image for automatic text extraction.

02

Text Processing

The text is transformed using TF-IDF vectorization with n-gram features to capture linguistic patterns.

03

ML Classification

A trained classifier evaluates the statement against thousands of verified and debunked claims.

04

Multi-Source Verification

Cross-references against 150,000+ news sources and performs deep semantic analysis for the final verdict.

About VerifiNews

In an age of information overload, distinguishing fact from fiction has become increasingly challenging. VerifiNews leverages natural language processing and machine learning to provide an automated first line of defense against misinformation.

Our model is trained on the LIAR benchmark dataset, which contains thousands of labeled real-world political statements, making it particularly effective at evaluating factual claims. VerifiNews cross-references claims against 150,000+ news sources and uses deep semantic analysis to deliver highly accurate verification results.
